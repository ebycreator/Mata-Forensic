About META FORENSIC

META FORENSIC is an Android-based Digital Forensics application designed to extract and analyze metadata from image files. The application generates cryptographic hashes, extracts EXIF metadata, identifies device information, retrieves GPS data, and creates downloadable forensic reports for investigations and evidence documentation.
